# MijiaLAN 0.4.1

## 简体中文

原生 macOS 版，Build 50。通用安装包支持 macOS 12 及以上，适用于 Apple 芯片和 Intel。

- 点击「重新导入」会用已保存的米家登录获取设备当前的局域网 token。登录过期时会显示二维码，扫码确认后自动继续导入。
- 空气净化器详情页会直接显示设备支持的 PM2.5、温度、湿度及滤芯剩余寿命。设备在线且能提供读数时，待机也能查看。
- 钥匙串暂时不可用或设备清单无法读取时，会保留原有清单。手动添加设备遇到保存失败时会提示重试。
- 已下载的设备规格会保存在本机。缺少规格时，应用会在启动、导入或重试时尝试恢复设备控件。
- 修复了 Apple「家庭」桥接设备接近数量上限时，缺少可用服务的设备占用名额、导致其他配件被遗漏的问题。

可在设置中检查更新，或从 GitHub Releases 下载经 Developer ID 签名和 Apple 公证的 DMG 或 ZIP。

## English

Native macOS release, build 50. The Universal installer supports macOS 12 and later on Apple silicon and Intel.

- Re-import Devices uses your saved Xiaomi Home login to fetch current LAN tokens. If your login has expired, scan the QR code and the import resumes automatically.
- Air purifier details now show the PM2.5, temperature, humidity, and remaining filter life reported by the device. Readings are also available on standby when the purifier is reachable and reports them.
- MijiaLAN preserves your saved device list if Keychain is temporarily unavailable or the list cannot be read. Adding a device manually now reports a save failure so you can retry.
- Downloaded device specifications are kept on your Mac. If a specification is missing, the app tries to restore the device controls at startup, on import, or when you retry.
- Fixed an issue in large Apple Home setups where devices with no available Home services could take up a bridge slot and leave other accessories out of Home.

Check for updates in Settings, or download the Developer ID signed and Apple notarized DMG or ZIP from GitHub Releases.
